# ECLIPSE — Draft retail pricing (MAD)

**Status: DRAFT — not approved.** None of these prices should go live before you sign off.
No confirmed prices were supplied with the brief, so every figure below is a proposal.

- Research date: **5 November 2026**
- Market: Morocco. All reference prices are **currently listed** prices in MAD (promo price shown first where the retailer displays a struck-through original).
- Method: proposed price sits between the local streetwear promo price and the original MSRP, rounded to the nearest 9. ECLIPSE is positioned as a small premium label, not fast fashion.
- To change a price: edit the `price` field for that product in `js/script.js`. Cards, modal, cart totals and the WhatsApp message all read from the same data.

## Notes before launch

- Retail prices in Morocco are usually shown as `499 MAD` (or `499,00 DH`). The storefront now uses `MAD`.
- Check shipping/COD cost before finalising: Originals offers free delivery over 499 MAD, DeFacto charges ~20–40 MAD. If you charge delivery separately on WhatsApp, keep the product prices as proposed.
- Discount strategy: the reference stores run 30–60% promos. The proposed prices are already below their struck-through MSRPs, so avoid stacking another launch discount on top.
